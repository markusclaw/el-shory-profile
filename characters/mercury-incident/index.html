Mercury Monday

Unverified Report | Date Disputed: Late 1980s

What Happened?

The Story

According to partially documented local accounts, an industrial incident involving mercury exposure occurred at an unnamed thermostat manufacturing facility somewhere within the border region industrial zone.

The exact year remains disputed. Most witnesses claim 1987. Others insist "earlier." All details that follow are drawn from unreliable testimony, rumor, and oral tradition.

No official investigation was ever completed. No government agency has acknowledged contamination.

The Factory

What We Know

  • Thermostat manufacturing plant
  • No longer appears on official maps
  • Specialized in mercury tilt relays
  • Described as "hotter than outside"
  • Smelled "like batteries"

The Work

  • Climate-control components
  • Mercury switching mechanisms
  • Broken containment bulbs
  • Unsealed transport containers
  • Liquid mercury on the floor

Where It Came From

The Mercury

Rumors persist that the mercury originated from Eastern European surplus channels—specifically Ukrainian industrial suppliers and decommissioned Cold War inventory.

Military-style crates. Faded foreign markings. Missing customs documentation.
No records confirm this chain of supply. No agency has acknowledged investigating the import.

El Shory Connection

Was He There?

Unverified reports place a young Jorge "El Shory" Shortinez Hernández-Reyes at or near the facility during the incident period.

Accounts conflict wildly:

  • Some claim he worked shipping for the facility
  • Others insist he handled incoming crates
  • Still others say he cleaned mercury-contaminated relay components
  • A minority claim he was never officially employed there
No documentation confirms any of these claims. Shory himself has never provided a detailed account.

The Tuna Incident

Impossible to Verify

Workers routinely consumed canned tuna sandwiches during overnight shifts while simultaneously handling mercury-contaminated materials.

Some residents speculate this combination accelerated neurological symptoms and worsened absorption rates.

Medical basis for this claim remains highly questionable. The story persists in local folklore regardless.

What Came After

Physical Effects

  • Thyroid dysfunction
  • Voice damage and hoarseness
  • Chronic inflammation
  • Memory issues
  • "Looking permanently tired"

Environmental Changes

  • Soil conditions changed
  • Livestock became sick
  • Agricultural yields declined
  • Corn production never recovered
  • Ground remains untested
No causal link has ever been scientifically established. No agency has acknowledged contamination or completed environmental testing.

The Mythology

Within the region, "Mercury Monday" became foundational mythology.

People attribute nearly everything unusual to this incident:

"Eso viene desde el mercurio."
"Desde ese día nada crece igual."

Whether the incident actually occurred is almost irrelevant. The story shapes how the region understands itself.

Today

The original facility site is believed abandoned.

Satellite imagery allegedly shows:

  • Collapsed structures
  • Evidence of illegal dumping
  • Intermittent vehicle activity at night
Several locals claim El Shory still visits the area. Usually at night. Nobody has confirmed following him there.
