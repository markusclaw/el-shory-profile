Part One: The Finder

El Shory

Nobody knows his real name. Nobody knows where he came from. All anyone knows is: call El Shory when you need something. Government equipment. Classified hardware. Military surplus. Industrial salvage. Anything. El Shory finds it. Nobody knows where. Nobody asks how. The van shows up, the item is there, and somehow the price is always reasonable. He doesn't sell things—he *locates* them. Impossibly. Consistently. Mysteriously.

The Mercury Incident of '87

At fourteen, El Shory found work at a chemical processing plant in an unnamed industrial border town. The facility's motto was simple: "Safety Third."

Three weeks into the job came what locals call Mercury Monday—an industrial "ventilation irregularity" that would reshape his life. Official records describe it as minor. Residents tell a different story: the day the pigeons forgot how to fly.

What happened next is documented only in whispers. Subcutaneous mercury contamination. Thyroid complications. A resistance to tetanus that shouldn't exist. The corn stopped growing correctly. Everyone in the region developed something—a sickness without a name. The mayor buried the reports before re-election.

None of it was ever officially proven. Which is exactly why people believe it.

The Finder

How It Works (Nobody Knows)

You call El Shory. You tell him what you need. "I need a military-grade signal jammer." "I need decommissioned lab equipment." "I need industrial robotics firmware."

He nods. Maybe says "Simón" if Pecas is there. Doesn't ask questions. Doesn't explain. Three days later, it appears. Exactly what you asked for. Working condition. No documentation. No questions asked. No receipt.

The mystery: Where does it come from? Government auctions? Seized equipment? Black market connections? Literal theft? He will never say. You will never know. It simply... exists in his possession, and now it can be yours.

His business card reads: "If it exists, I know a guy." (The guy is him.)

Documented Acquisitions

Things El Shory Has "Found":

  • Decommissioned military communications equipment (origin unknown)
  • Industrial robotics components (source unverified)
  • Classified government hardware (definitely unethical)
  • Medical-grade machinery (somehow functional)
  • Semiconductor manufacturing tools (illegality suspected)
  • Encrypted data servers (questions best not asked)

The joke: He doesn't steal. He doesn't smuggle. He simply has an inexplicable ability to locate items that should not be locatable. Is it miraculous? Is it criminal? Is it both? El Shory will never clarify.

Accomplice

El Pecas

The silent companion. The man with the freckles, the deadpan stare, and the inexplicable loyalty. El Pecas speaks in nods, sunflower seeds, and the single word "Simón." Yet somehow, in his quiet presence, more is understood than in a thousand explanations. Where El Shory talks, Pecas listens. Where Shory gestures, Pecas acts. He is the counterweight to chaos.

The Silent Partner

Communication Method

El Pecas is a man of few (if any) words. His vocabulary consists of:

  • Nodding — Agreement, acknowledgment, understanding
  • Sunflower seeds — Contemplation, patience, presence
  • "Simón" — Affirmation, solidarity, "I'm here"

Yet people listen to him more than they listen to most who speak.

Physical Profile

Appearance

  • Freckles across face (hence the alias)
  • "PECAS" tattoo on neck
  • Gold chain necklace
  • Black shirt (always El Shory branded)
  • Deadpan, knowing expression
  • Digital watch (black, utilitarian)

Relationship to El Shory

Inseparable. Where one goes, the other follows. El Shory talks, El Pecas nods. El Shory philosophizes, El Pecas understands. The dynamic is unspoken but absolute—a partnership of opposites that somehow functions as a single unit.

The White Van

1998 Chevy Astro

The mobile headquarters. No plates. One mismatched door. Passenger window non-functional. Rear suspension that squeaks like tortured dolphins. Somehow always already parked at the destination before they arrive.

Critical anomaly: Nobody has ever witnessed El Shory or El Pecas purchase fuel. Yet the van runs. Where does it get gas? How does it move? These are questions best left unasked.

"PUBLIC SOURCE SPECIALIST" — painted on the side

The Mystery

Personality

  • Unnervingly calm about the impossible
  • Never explains his methods
  • Never justifies his sources
  • Never apologizes for ethics
  • Always delivers exactly what you asked for

The Real Question

Is El Shory a criminal? A savant? A connected insider? Nobody knows. He simply exists in the space between legal and illegal, ethical and unethical, possible and impossible. And he always—always—finds what you need.

When Asked Direct Questions

"Where does it come from?"
El Shory: [nods]
"How did you find this?"
El Shory: [adjusts cap] "Connections."
"Is this... legal?"
El Shory: "Does it matter?" [It does. But he won't say.]
"How much?"
El Shory: [quotes a price that is somehow always fair] "Take it or leave it."
"Can you get...?"
El Shory: "Probably. Give me three days."
