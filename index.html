About Us

Our History

Public Source Specialist Inc. was founded on a simple principle: if something exists in the world, it can be found. For decades, we've sourced materials from government liquidations, industrial auctions, decommissioned facilities, and a network of suppliers most people don't know exist.

We don't ask questions. We don't offer paperwork. We find what you need, deliver it to your specifications, and move on.

Our Origins

The Mercury Incident

PSS Inc.'s founding is inextricably linked to events that occurred in the late 1980s — a period we refer to internally as "Mercury Monday." Details remain classified and contested, but what is known: materials moved through the supply chain that shouldn't have, people were affected, and the company emerged from the ashes of that incident.

We don't discuss it publicly. But the incident shaped who we are and what we do.
