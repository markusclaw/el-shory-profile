Part One: The Talker

El Shory

Jorge "El Shory" Shortinez Hernández-Reyes is a figure of legend in the industrial borderlands. A survivor of the Mercury Incident of '87, material acquisition specialist, and keeper of forgotten truths. His story exists at the intersection of myth and reality—a man whose past is written in scars and whose present is a rolling white van filled with salvage and secrets.

The Mercury Incident of '87

At fourteen, El Shory found work at a chemical processing plant in an unnamed industrial border town. The facility's motto was simple: "Safety Third."

Three weeks into the job came what locals call Mercury Monday—an industrial "ventilation irregularity" that would reshape his life. Official records describe it as minor. Residents tell a different story: the day the pigeons forgot how to fly.

What happened next is documented only in whispers. Subcutaneous mercury contamination. Thyroid complications. A resistance to tetanus that shouldn't exist. The corn stopped growing correctly. Everyone in the region developed something—a sickness without a name. The mayor buried the reports before re-election.

None of it was ever officially proven. Which is exactly why people believe it.

Enterprise

Material Acquisition Specialist

El Shory operates an independent procurement operation, specializing in what he calls "public-source materials." His business card reads simply: "If it exists, I know a guy."

Specializations:

  • Recycled metals and industrial salvage
  • Surplus electronics and reclaimed components
  • "Public-source" procurement (ethics unverified)
  • Custom hardware assembly and integration

The Dongle Era

In the underground hardware scene, El Shory rose to prominence manufacturing "Slave Dongles"—compact controller devices assembled from reclaimed aluminum, retired government hardware, abandoned USB hubs, and materials sourced from "public containers." When questioned about their legality, his response became legendary:

"No es robado si nadie lo está usando."
— On acquisition ethics
Accomplice

El Pecas

The silent companion. The man with the freckles, the deadpan stare, and the inexplicable loyalty. El Pecas speaks in nods, sunflower seeds, and the single word "Simón." Yet somehow, in his quiet presence, more is understood than in a thousand explanations. Where El Shory talks, Pecas listens. Where Shory gestures, Pecas acts. He is the counterweight to chaos.

The Silent Partner

Communication Method

El Pecas is a man of few (if any) words. His vocabulary consists of:

  • Nodding — Agreement, acknowledgment, understanding
  • Sunflower seeds — Contemplation, patience, presence
  • "Simón" — Affirmation, solidarity, "I'm here"

Yet people listen to him more than they listen to most who speak.

Physical Profile

Relationship to El Shory

Inseparable. Where one goes, the other follows. El Shory talks, El Pecas nods. El Shory philosophizes, El Pecas understands. The dynamic is unspoken but absolute—a partnership of opposites that somehow functions as a single unit.

The White Van

1998 Chevy Astro

The mobile headquarters. No plates. One mismatched door. Passenger window non-functional. Rear suspension that squeaks like tortured dolphins. Somehow always already parked at the destination before they arrive.

Critical anomaly: Nobody has ever witnessed El Shory or El Pecas purchase fuel. Yet the van runs. Where does it get gas? How does it move? These are questions best left unasked.

"PUBLIC SOURCE SPECIALIST" — painted on the side
